2 question:Write a program that reads a floating-point number and prints successively rounded values, reducing the number of decimal places by one each step.

Input Format:

A single floating-point number.

Output Format:

Print each step on a separate line.

Rounded to 5 decimal places

Rounded to 4 decimal places

Rounded to 3 decimal places

Rounded to 2 decimal places

Rounded to 1 decimal place

Integer part only

Smaple Input :
34.12345

Smaple Output :
34.12345
34.1235
34.123
34.12
34.1
34

solu:
import struct
a=float(input())
a=struct.unpack('f',struct.pack('f',a))[0]
print("%.5f"%a)
print("%.4f"%a)
print("%.3f"%a)
print("%.2f"%a)
print("%.1f"%a)
print("%.0f"%a)

11 question:Shape Area Calculation
Max Score: 10 | Difficulty: easy

Veena wants to learn how to calculate the area of different shapes (Square, Rectangle, Circle, Triangle) and implement it in programming.
Write a program to calculate the area of each shape based on the given inputs.

Formulas:

Square:Area=a×a
Rectangle:Area=l×b
Circle:Area=πr^2 (Use π = 3.14, round to 2 decimal places)
Triangle:Area=(1/2)×b×h
Input Format:

First input: Integer for side of square (a)
Second and third input: Integers for length (l) and breadth (b) of rectangle
Fourth input: Integer for radius (r) of circle
Fifth and sixth input: Integers for base (B) and height (h) of triangle
Constraints:
No constraints

Output Format:
Print the area of each shape in the following format:

Area of Square=...
Area of Rectangle=...
Area of Circle=...
Area of Triangle=...
Sample Input 0:
2
3
2
3
6
5

Sample Output 0:
Area of Square=4
Area of Rectangle=6
Area of Circle=28.26
Area of Triangle=15


solu:
side=int(input())
l=int(input())
b=int(input())
r=int(input())
B=int(input())
H=int(input())
sqr=side*side
rec=l*b
cir=3.14*pow(r,2)
tri=0.5*B*H
print(f"Area of Square={sqr}")
print(f"Area of Rectangle={rec}")
print(f"Area of Circle={cir:.2f}")
print(f"Area of Triangle={tri:.0f}")
